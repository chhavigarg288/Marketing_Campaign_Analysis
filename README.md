# Marketing_Campaign_Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a marketing campaign dataset to understand **customer behavior, spending patterns, and campaign effectiveness**. The goal of the analysis is to identify which customer segments contribute the most revenue and how different factors such as income, marital status, education, age group, and family structure influence purchasing behavior.

The project follows a **complete data analytics workflow**, including data cleaning, transformation, SQL analysis, and dashboard visualization.

Tools used in this project include **Excel, SQL, and Power BI**.

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were:

* Analyze customer demographics and spending behavior
* Evaluate marketing campaign performance
* Identify high-value customer segments
* Understand product category revenue distribution
* Compare sales channels (Store, Web, Catalog)
* Build an interactive marketing analytics dashboard

# Data Cleaning Process (Excel)

Data cleaning and preprocessing were performed in Excel before moving to SQL and Power BI.

### 1. Handling Missing Values

* Checked dataset for missing values
* Ensured important fields like income and purchase metrics were valid

### 2. Age Calculation

Age was calculated using the `Year_Birth` column.

Since the dataset contains data up to **2014**, age was calculated using:

Age = 2014 − Year_Birth

### 3. Created Age Groups

Customers were grouped into age segments:

* 18–25
* 26–35
* 36–45
* 46–55
* 56–65
* 65+

This helps analyze spending patterns across different age groups.

### 4. Marital Status Refinement

Original marital status values contained multiple categories such as:

* Married
* Together
* Single
* Divorced
* Widow

These were simplified into broader groups:

* Couple
* Single

This allowed easier customer segmentation.

### 5. Total Kids Column

A new column **Total_Kids** was created:

Total_Kids = Kidhome + Teenhome

This helps analyze how family size affects spending behavior.

### 6. Total Spending Calculation

Total spending was calculated by summing all product category purchases:

Total_Spending =
MntWines + MntFruits + MntMeatProducts +
MntFishProducts + MntSweetProducts + MntGoldProds

This represents total revenue generated by each customer.

### 7. Income Segmentation

Customers were grouped into:

* Low Income
* Middle Income
* High Income

This allowed analysis of spending behavior by income level.

### 8. Excel Analysis

Pivot tables were created to analyze:

* Spending by marital status
* Spending by education
* Spending by income group
* Spending by number of kids

A KPI dashboard was also created in Excel before moving to Power BI.


# SQL Analysis

SQL was used to further analyze customer behavior and segment the data.

Example analyses performed:

* Spending by marital status
* Spending by income group
* Revenue by product category
* Campaign response rate
* Customer segmentation

Example query to calculate response rate:

```
SELECT 
(COUNT(CASE WHEN Response = 1 THEN 1 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(*)) AS response_rate
FROM marketing_campaign;
```

This calculates the percentage of customers who accepted the marketing campaign

# Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important insights:

1. High-income customers contribute the majority of total revenue.

2. Couples without children show higher spending compared to families with multiple kids.

3. Wine and meat products generate the highest revenue among all product categories.

4. Customers with higher education levels tend to spend more.

5. Store purchases dominate compared to catalog and web purchases.

These insights can help businesses improve **customer targeting and marketing strategies**.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis of customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and campaign performance, the following strategic recommendations can help improve marketing effectiveness and increase revenue.

## 1. Target High-Income Customers

High-income customers contribute the majority of total spending. Marketing campaigns should prioritize this segment by:

* Offering premium product bundles
* Providing exclusive promotions and loyalty rewards
* Personalizing marketing emails and offers

Focusing on high-income segments can significantly improve overall campaign ROI.


## 2. Focus on Couples Without Children

The analysis shows that **couples without children spend more than families with multiple kids**.

Recommended strategies:

* Create targeted campaigns for couples
* Offer lifestyle-oriented products
* Promote premium and luxury products

This segment represents a **high-value customer group**.


## 3. Promote High-Performing Product Categories

Wine and meat products generate the highest revenue among all categories.

Recommended actions:

* Bundle high-demand products with complementary items
* Run targeted promotions on top-performing products
* Highlight these products in marketing campaigns

This can further increase overall sales.


## 4. Improve Digital Sales Channels

Store purchases dominate compared to web and catalog purchases.

To improve online performance:

* Offer exclusive online discounts
* Improve website user experience
* Launch targeted digital marketing campaigns

Increasing web purchases can reduce operational costs and scale sales more efficiently.


## 5. Personalize Campaigns Based on Customer Segments

Different customer groups show different spending behaviors.

Marketing campaigns should be segmented based on:

* Income group
* Age group
* Education level
* Family size

Personalized campaigns generally produce **higher response rates and better engagement**.

## 6. Improve Campaign Conversion Rate

The campaign response rate is approximately **15%**, which indicates potential for improvement.

Strategies to increase conversion:

* A/B testing marketing messages
* Personalized offers based on past purchase behavior
* Time-limited promotional campaigns

Even a small increase in response rate could significantly increase revenue.


## Final Strategic Insight

Data-driven customer segmentation allows businesses to identify **high-value customers and optimize marketing strategies**. By focusing on the most profitable segments and improving digital channels, companies can significantly enhance campaign effectiveness and overall revenue growth.
